# Landing Page Enhancements - EcoSort

## Overview
Comprehensive information sections have been added to the EcoSort landing page to help users understand how the system works, the process, and how to use the platform.

---

## New Sections Added

### 1. **How EcoSort Works** 
Located right after the role selection cards, this section provides a visual 4-step process flow:

**Step 1: Schedule Pickup** 
- Citizens book waste collection through the app
- Choose preferred date and time
- Blue-themed card with step number

**Step 2: Route Optimization**
- System assigns requests to dealers
- Automatic route optimization
- Orange-themed card

**Step 3: Collection & Sorting**
- Dealers collect and sort waste
- Real-time status updates
- Green-themed card

**Step 4: Track & Reward**
- Citizens earn EcoPoints
- Admins monitor performance
- Purple-themed card

---

### 2. **Role-Based Usage Guide**
Three detailed cards explaining how to use each portal:

#### For Citizens (5 Steps)
1. Create account and complete profile
2. Schedule pickup with waste details
3. Track collection status in real-time
4. Earn EcoPoints and redeem rewards
5. View history and recycling impact

#### For Dealers (5 Steps)
1. Register and get admin approval
2. View assigned collection schedule
3. Use map view for route optimization
4. Update pickup status and waste weight
5. Track performance and earnings

#### For Admins (5 Steps)
1. Monitor system-wide operations
2. Approve and manage dealer accounts
3. Configure zones and assign dealers
4. Analyze waste collection trends
5. Generate reports and insights

---

### 3. **Quick Start Guide**
Eye-catching emerald gradient card with 3 simple steps:

**Step 1: Choose Your Role**
- Select from Citizen, Dealer, or Admin portal

**Step 2: Create Account**
- Sign up with details or use demo credentials

**Step 3: Start Managing**
- Schedule pickups, track collections, or monitor operations

Includes a prominent "Get Started Now" call-to-action button.

---

### 4. **What We Collect** (Waste Types)
Four category cards showing accepted waste types:

#### Organic Waste (Green)
- Food scraps
- Yard waste
- Compostable materials

#### Recyclables (Blue)
- Paper
- Plastic
- Glass
- Metal items

#### E-Waste (Orange)
- Electronics
- Batteries
- Appliances

#### Hazardous (Red)
- Chemicals
- Paints
- Medical waste

---

### 5. **FAQ Section**
Five comprehensive question-answer cards covering:

**Q1: How do I schedule a waste pickup?**
- Step-by-step guidance for scheduling
- Login → Navigate → Select → Confirm

**Q2: What are EcoPoints and how do I earn them?**
- Explanation of reward system
- Ways to earn and redeem points

**Q3: Which areas does EcoSort serve?**
- Coverage information
- Expansion plans

**Q4: How do dealers get assigned to collection requests?**
- Smart routing system explanation
- Assignment criteria

**Q5: What are the pickup time slots available?**
- Morning: 6 AM - 10 AM
- Afternoon: 12 PM - 4 PM
- Evening: 4 PM - 8 PM
